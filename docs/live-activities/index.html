Live Activity gallery

Every presentation of SportsCal’s iOS Live Activity, rendered from the Gallery · previews in LiveSportActivityWidget.swift with Xcode’s RenderPreview. The renders are from an iPhone 18 Pro simulator; the Duo row is from an iPhone Duo. Images are native 3× pixels, and the island slots sit on a dark stage so they stay legible.

Lock ScreenThe banner on the Lock Screen and in Notification Center.
StandByThe same view scaled up while the phone is charging on its side.
Smart StackThe Apple Watch Smart Stack, which uses the small activity family.
Island expandedShown after a long-press on the Dynamic Island, or briefly when an update alerts.
Island compactThe leading and trailing pills on either side of the camera when one activity is running.
MinimalThe small bubble next to the camera when two activities run at once. On the iPhone Duo, it’s also what fills the outer-screen camera cut-out.
LandscapeThe compact and minimal slots rotated with the phone.

Problems found

Fixed in a4949a5

  • Compact leads with the leader. nfl-compact is now “KC 20-17” and nbafinal-compact is “BOS 121-117”; the leading score comes first. The landscape compacts match.
  • LIVE badge shows only while play runs. soccerht-expanded shows just HALF and nbafinal-expanded just FINAL/OT.
  • Overtime finals say so. nbafinal-lock and nbafinal-expanded read FINAL/OT.
  • Expanded badges use team codes: BOS, EDM, DET, CLE, BUF and KC instead of BCE, EOI, DTI, CGU, BBI and KCC.
  • F1 minimal shows the leader (“PIA” in f1-minimal) instead of a bare flag. The golf minimal fix wasn’t captured (see the note below).
  • Golf round header appears once (“• Round 3” in golf-lock).

Still open

  • Smart Stack: the ActivityConfiguration needs .supplementalActivityFamilies([.small]), and Xcode won’t render that slot without it.
  • Compact landscape for single-player sports is just “icon …” on top (golf, F1 and tennis compact-landscape).
  • Expanded island has a large empty bottom in team-sport expanded views.
  • Tennis compact has no names (“🎾 1-1 … 3-2”).
  • Tennis minimal doesn’t say whose sets it shows (“1-1”).

Re-rendered on 2026-11-20 after a4949a5: 83 of 103 slots have images. The 10 Smart Stack slots can’t render (see above), and 10 slots weren’t re-captured: golf Lock Screen dark, StandBy, expanded (both), minimal and minimal landscape; F1 StandBy and minimal landscape; NBA final Lock Screen dark and StandBy. Their earlier images showed pre-fix behavior, so I removed them. The preview simulator keeps several live activities alive, so the island slots (and sometimes the content views) often render a different scenario than the one requested. I checked every image by eye and filed it under the scenario it actually shows. The island ignores color scheme (light and dark expanded renders were pixel-identical in 6 of 6 pairs), so nbafinal-expanded-dark reuses the light render. Team-sport minimal and the iPhone Duo minimal didn’t change in a4949a5; those images are the earlier verified renders.
