  • Search is the whole funnel: 59 of 78 first-time downloads came from App Store search, 14 from app referrers, 4 from browse and 1 from the web. The US is about 40% of downloads, then GB, CA and PH.
  • Two problems, both fixable: (1) visibility: 5.4k impressions in 5.5 weeks means we rank for very few searches people actually make; (2) conversion: 2.5% of people who see us tap. The live screenshots are still the Nov 2022 "SportsCal"-branded art, so they don't match the app's name, and there's no 6.9" set.
  • Retention leak: about 1 in 3 installs gets deleted. Growth spent on acquisition is wasted until activation is better.
  • Listing debt: the live description advertises $2.99 / $19.99 / $39.99, but the live prices are $0.99 / $9.99 and Lifetime has never been reviewed. 3.3 is sitting in PREPARE_FOR_SUBMISSION.
Goal for the next 4 months: get to 100+ first-time downloads a week by the end of March (10×), with day-7 deletes under 20%. Every number gets checked against the appstore telemetry channel and the ASC reports, which now generate daily.

Phase 1 · Activation and retention weeks 2–5

  • Instrument the first session: add telemetry for onboarding_complete, first_favorite, first_widget_added, first_live_activity, notifications_granted, plus a server-side D1/D7 return rate built from the DAU HLLs. Without these numbers we can't tell why a third of users delete the app.
  • Onboarding gets to "aha" in under 60 seconds: pick teams → see their next game → one tap to follow it with a Live Activity or reminder → nudge to add the widget. The "follow team" step is the retention hook.
  • Empty-state audit: a user who installs on a quiet day (no games for their sports) should still see upcoming games and the calendar, never a blank screen.
  • Re-engagement: a weekly "your teams this week" local notification, opt-in, free tier.

How we'll measure

  • Weekly: an ASC pull (downloads by source/territory, impressions → taps → installs, deletes) using the scripts from this session, plus telemetry DAU/WAU for the appstore channel.
  • Change one listing variable at a time, e.g. screenshots before keywords, ~2 weeks apart, so the effect is readable even at small volume.
  • Optional later: a small Apple Search Ads budget, used only to find which keywords convert, then put those into organic.
